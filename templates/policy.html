Security and privacy policy

Security

Published RPMs, DEBs, and repository metadata are signed before release. Use the public signing key, checksums, and immutable image digests to verify the artifacts you consume.

Report security issues privately through the contact or security process associated with the packaging repository; do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.

Privacy

This repository site is a read-only distribution service. It does not request account information or intentionally collect personal content. Normal infrastructure logs may include request metadata needed to operate and protect the service.

Updates

This policy may be updated as the service and its publication infrastructure evolve.
